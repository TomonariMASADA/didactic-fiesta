With respect to pain, it is irrelevant whether the pain is private or not. It is relevant why the pain is not on the other side in some cases.

When there is a pain, the whole world aches according to our indifference. However, the pain is not on the other side in some cases. This is an experience of Grenze, from which the privateness of pain derives.

The privateness of pain derives from specific experiences of Grenze, where there is a pain but not on the other side.

Generally speaking, all qualia can be discussed in the same manner as pain.

Qualia are generally derived as follows:

1. According to our indifference, the world is as we see it.
2. However, there is someone who reacts differently to that world. This leads us to realize that some do not see the world as we do. Yet, we cannot know how they actually see it.
3. This implies that we, too, see the world in the same manner as that person. Since we know we are currently seeing something red, we grasp what might be called the qualia of redness at this very moment.

In this way, qualia are derived through multiple steps; they are by no means grasped through immediate experience. For the world that is immediately experienced is simply the world as it is experienced. What is referred to as the privateness of qualia merely derives from step 2 above. It is by no means something that we directly perceive regarding our own qualia.
